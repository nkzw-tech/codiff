# GPT-6 walkthrough evaluation — 2026-09-24

The current production defaults remain the best supported choices for medium and large walkthroughs. GPT-6 Sol at low reasoning is promising for small walkthroughs, but the suite has only two small commits, so this run does not justify changing the default for every small change.

## Method

- Ran all four fixed cases twice through Codiff's production Codex path for each model and reasoning setting. The baseline used production size routing: GPT-5.6 Terra at low reasoning below 100 reviewable hunks, GPT-5.5 at low reasoning at 100 or more.
- Tested GPT-6 Astra, Sol, and Luna at low and medium reasoning. All 56 generation attempts succeeded, and none fell back to another model.
- Scored each saved walkthrough with `evals/judge.mjs`, which checks the commit with read-only git commands and applies the case rubric. The judge uses the local Codex CLI default model. Quality is an average of two 0–100 scores; generation time is the median of two attempts. Times can vary with service load.
- Full prompts, responses, metrics, judge notes, and reports are in the ignored `evals/runs/` directories named below.

## Decision

- **Small:** GPT-6 Sol at low reasoning improved both cases by 6 and 2 points. The focused case lost some main-path hunk coverage (56% versus 61%), and two commits are too few to establish a reliable default across small changes. Keep Sol available by explicit model ID for further evaluation, and gather more cases before adding it to the Model menu or routing small walkthroughs to it automatically.
- **Medium:** Keep GPT-5.6 Terra at low reasoning. Its 83.5 quality score exceeded every GPT-6 setting, and it was faster than all GPT-6 medium-case runs.
- **Large:** Keep GPT-5.5 at low reasoning. Its 75.0 quality score exceeded every GPT-6 setting. The best GPT-6 quality was Sol at medium reasoning (73.5) with a 35.4s median, compared with 22.6s for GPT-5.5.

The judge repeatedly found that candidate medium and large walkthroughs omitted fallback behavior, central review interactions, or regression tests. These are the decisive quality gaps, beyond the numeric scores.
